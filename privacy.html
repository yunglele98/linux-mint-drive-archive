Privacy policy

Linux Mint Drive Archive · Effective October 13, 2026

This is a personal desktop OAuth application operated by the owner of the Google account and Linux Mint computer. It is used locally with rclone, not as a hosted service for other users.

Data access and purpose

With the owner's explicit Google authorization, rclone may access Google Drive files and metadata to list, upload, download and verify personal archives. Its requested Drive scope also permits editing and deleting files; those actions occur only when the owner runs or authorizes corresponding commands.

Storage and transfers

Files transfer directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration and private local configuration backups. File names, sizes and checksums may be retained in local verification manifests and logs. Archived files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them.

There is no separate application database or developer-hosted server receiving Google user data. This public information website does not receive Drive files or OAuth tokens. Google processes stored files under its own policies; GitHub processes ordinary website request information under its own policies.

Sharing and use

Google user data is used only for the owner's requested file-management and archive operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to unrelated parties, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence models. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections, remove local rclone tokens and backups, or delete archived files from Google Drive. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but does not itself remove existing archives or local manifests.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner through the project issue tracker. Do not submit credentials, tokens, private files or other sensitive information to the public tracker.
